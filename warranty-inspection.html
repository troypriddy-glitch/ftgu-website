Why Month 11

Your Last Chance to Make the Builder Pay.

Most new homeowners don't think about their builder warranty until it's too late. The 11th month is your window — before the warranty closes and repair costs become yours alone.

🕐

Timing Is Everything

Builder warranties typically expire at 12 months. An inspection at month 11 gives you the documentation and time to submit warranty claims before the deadline hits.

📋

Documented Findings, Not Just a List

Troy's Spectora report provides photos, descriptions, and clear documentation of every deficiency — giving you a professional record to submit directly to your builder's warranty department.

🏗️

Settlement & Shifting

After 12 months of SETX weather, heat cycles, and soil movement, settlement issues become visible. Troy documents cracking, door frame shifts, and foundation movement indicators.

💧

Water Intrusion & Moisture

The first year of rain often reveals roofing, flashing, and drainage deficiencies that weren't apparent at final walk. Year-one moisture issues are still warranty-covered.

⚡

Mechanical System Performance

A full year of HVAC, plumbing, and electrical operation surfaces performance issues that weren't detectable at closing. These are warranty-eligible defects.

🤝

Negotiation Leverage

A professional inspection report carries more weight with a builder's warranty department than a homeowner's complaint list. Troy's documentation is designed to hold up under pushback.
