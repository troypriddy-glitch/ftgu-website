Why Month 11?

Most builder warranties cover workmanship for one year. Once it expires, repairs are on you.

Settlement, roof, plumbing, and HVAC issues often show up during the first year as your home goes through a full cycle of seasons.

Our report gives you clear, photo-documented findings you can submit directly to your builder.

What We Check

  • Foundation and signs of settlement
  • Roof, attic, and drainage
  • Electrical system
  • Plumbing
  • Heating and air conditioning
  • Windows, doors, and interior finishes
  • Exterior grading and water management
