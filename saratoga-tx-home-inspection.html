Plan Your Inspection

Pier and beam access

If a Saratoga home has a crawlspace, tell us about the access opening and any known clearance or standing-water issues. FTGU has a crawl bot available for accessible pier and beam spaces. Conditions determine how much can be observed; concealed framing and unreachable areas remain limitations.

Choose services for your property

If the property has a private well, septic system, or pier and beam foundation, identify those systems in the quote request. Water sampling, septic services, and additional foundation evaluation should be selected for the actual property.

FTGU’s general inspection addresses accessible roofing, structure, electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling systems, and other components within the agreed scope. It is a visual, non-destructive inspection, with limitations for concealed or inaccessible areas.

Foundation questions deserve context

A visual foundation inspection and an elevation measurement service answer different questions. FTGU offers foundation elevation measurements and more extensive evaluations through Noble Engineering. A crawl bot is available for pier and beam homes where access permits. Engineering opinions are provided by the engineer.

A report you can use

Your digital report includes photos and explanations of observed concerns, their significance, and next actions. You and your agent can use Spectora’s Repair Request Builder to organize selected findings. FTGU reports the condition; your agent helps with contract decisions, and contractors provide repair estimates.

  • Share the full address, approximate size, and transaction deadline.
  • Identify private utilities, additional buildings, and requested add-ons.
  • Arrange utilities and access to the attic, panel, equipment, and foundation.
  • Provide repair records or specific concerns you want discussed.

Saratoga Inspection Questions

Can the crawl bot inspect every crawlspace?

No. Opening size, clearance, obstructions, water, and other site conditions affect access. Discuss the foundation type and any known access problems before booking.

How much does an inspection in Saratoga cost?

Use our online booking page or call for property-specific pricing. Provide the full address, square footage, property type, and desired services, then confirm the total and included scope before completing your booking.
