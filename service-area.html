Local Expertise

Southeast Texas Properties Are Different.

The Gulf Coast climate, soil conditions, and construction history of Southeast Texas create inspection challenges that out-of-area inspectors simply don't understand. Troy has been inspecting these specific conditions since 2004.

🏗️

Pier & Beam Foundations

A large portion of Southeast Texas homes sit on pier and beam foundations — more common here than in most Texas markets. Moisture, wood rot, settlement, and cross-bracing conditions require specialized knowledge.

💧

Gulf Coast Humidity

Extreme humidity accelerates wood deterioration, mold growth, and HVAC demand in ways that standard inspection frameworks don't capture. Troy evaluates moisture conditions with regional context.

🌪️

Hurricane Retrofit Conditions

Post-storm repairs, insurance-driven replacements, and hurricane retrofit requirements create unique documentation and condition issues throughout the Golden Triangle that require local knowledge to evaluate correctly.

🐜

Formosan Termite Pressure

Southeast Texas is one of the highest-risk regions in the U.S. for Formosan subterranean termites — among the most destructive invasive species in North America. WDI inspection is essential for every SETX buyer.

🏭

Industrial Corridor Properties

Properties near Beaumont's industrial corridor have specific inspection considerations related to prior use, environmental adjacency, and equipment infrastructure that require familiarity with the region's industrial history.

🏡

20+ Years of SETX Neighborhoods

Troy has inspected homes in virtually every neighborhood across Jefferson, Hardin, and Orange Counties. Builder patterns, era-specific conditions, and neighborhood-level issues are part of his inspection approach.
